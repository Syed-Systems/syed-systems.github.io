About ASME

What the American Society of Mechanical Engineers does for students and working engineers.

American Society of Mechanical Engineers

It is a not-for-profit professional organization that helps students and engineers exchange knowledge, develop skills, and grow professionally. It also provides engineering codes and standards, publications, conferences, and educational resources.

At IUT, the student section brings that learning spirit into campus life.

Frequently asked questions

Is ASME only about mechanical engineering?

ASME brings students and professionals together to share knowledge and develop skills across engineering disciplines. Ask the IUT Student Section about any specific membership criteria.

What can I do in a student section?

Our activities include orientations, alumni knowledge sharing, technical writing, poster contests, industrial visits and community outreach. The exact upcoming calendar may differ.

Are there opportunities to present my own ideas?

Yes. PIXELATE was a digital poster presentation competition, and the inter-university Article Writing Competition covered STEM topics including nanotechnology and composite materials. STEM Chronicles also publishes undergraduate writing.

How does this connect to real engineering work?

The 25 July 2026 visit to Walton Hi-Tech Industries PLC gave section members firsthand exposure to large-scale manufacturing. It is an example of connecting theory learned at university with practice in industry.

Does ASME provide scholarships?

The ASME Foundation offers scholarships for engineering students. Eligibility and awards depend on the current scholarship program.

How can I connect with ASME IUT?

Use the social channels in the footer to ask the student section about membership, upcoming events and ways to take part.
